Report Sheet Assessment Format

Use this as the score-entry guide.

JSS1 – JSS3

  • CA1 = 10 marks
  • CA2 = 10 marks
  • TEST1 = 20 marks
  • TEST2 = 20 marks
  • EXAM = 40 marks

SS1 – SS3

  • 1ST ASS. = 5 marks
  • 2ND ASS. = 5 marks
  • TEST = 20 marks
  • EXAM = 70 marks

Starter / Nursery / Primary / Tahfeez

  • Student rosters and subjects are active.
  • Attendance is active.
  • CA/Test entry is protected until the school's official assessment structure is supplied.
  • No SS scoring rule is automatically applied to these classes.
